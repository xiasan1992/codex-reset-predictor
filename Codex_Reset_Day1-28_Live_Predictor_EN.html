Forecast model & sensitivity

Transparent & adjustable
A first-order transition model uses recorded reset / improvement days: P(Reset | prior Reset) and P(Reset | prior Improvement). Each uses Beta(2,2) smoothing to avoid small-sample estimates of 0% or 100%.
8%
Poll adjustment = coefficient × (reset vote share − 50%). At the default setting, the adjustment is at most about ±4 percentage points. This coefficient is chosen manually, not fitted to data.
